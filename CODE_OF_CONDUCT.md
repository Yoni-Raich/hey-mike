# Code of Conduct

We want Hey Mike discussions and contributions to be safe, useful, and
respectful.

Expected behavior:

- Be kind and direct.
- Discuss ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Report problems privately when public details could create harm.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
privacy violations, doxxing, and sharing credentials or private device data.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when needed to protect
the community. Report conduct concerns through the private security/reporting
route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), without posting sensitive data.
